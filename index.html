How much AI work will you do?

Start with the size of the job, then tell us what kind of work it is. Routine work gets cheap once we tune for it. One-off work stays expensive. The split between the two moves your bill more than the total does, and it is the part most budgets get wrong.

How much work, in a year

billion tokens a year 5.0 trillion
500B30,000B

A token is about three quarters of a word, so a billion tokens is roughly 750 million words. Most mid-size companies running AI agents get through 2,000 to 8,000 billion of them in a year. If you have no idea yet, start at 5,000 and move the slider. Below 1,000 billion we will tell you to stay on AWS, because at that size we cannot beat their price.

Where the savings are

You pay for your machines around the clock, and you work them hard for about eight hours a day. We spend the quiet hours teaching small specialist models to handle your routine jobs, using your own work as the lesson. They then do those jobs for a fraction of what a general model charges. The teaching costs you nothing, because the machines were already yours and already sitting idle.
